Year: 2024
Journal: Journal of Ambient Intelligence and Smart Environments
Volume: 16(4), 409–438

### 7. Smart Hospital / Healthcare IoT

Paper: Internet of medical things: A systematic review
Year: 2023
Journal: Neurocomputing
DOI: 10.1016/j.neucom.2023.126719
This is one of the best foundation papers for your project. It reviews IoMT architectures, sensors, smart hospitals, health monitoring and challenges. It specifically discusses smart devices as nodes that collect health data and transmit it for processing.
CAREGRID connection:
Sensor nodes → Gateway → Processing → Hospital dashboard

### 8. mmWave / Radar Fall Detection


Paper: Radar-Based Fall Detection: A Survey
Authors: Shuting Hu, Siyang Cao, Nima Toosizadeh, Jennifer Barton, Melvin G. Hector, Mindy J. Fain
Year: 2024
Journal: IEEE Robotics & Automation Magazine
Volume: 31(3), 170–185
DOI: 10.1109/MRA.2024.3352851
This is an excellent reference for your mmWave/radar sensing. The survey covers radar-based fall detection, Micro-Doppler, Range-Doppler and Range-Doppler-Angle approaches, as well as privacy and real-world deployment challenges.
