# CAREGRID Research

This directory contains the research evidence used during the design and development of CAREGRID.

## Structure

### papers/

Original research papers and authoritative reports studied by the team.

### references/

Bibliographic metadata and source links.

### literature_review.md

Summary of the research areas studied and their relevance to CAREGRID.

### research_contribution.md

Mapping between literature findings and CAREGRID design decisions.

### source_links.md

Verified links to papers, organizations and supporting sources.

## Research Method

The research process followed:

1. Identify a CAREGRID design problem
2. Search for relevant literature
3. Compare existing approaches
4. Identify useful findings
5. Map findings to a design decision
6. Record the decision in the literature review
7. Identify limitations and future validation requirements
